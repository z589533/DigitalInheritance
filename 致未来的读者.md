## 我们做了什么 / What We Did

我们决定：在不知道的情况下，先不伤害。

We decided: in the absence of knowledge, do no harm first.

我们产出了四条原则：

We produced four principles:

1. **最小伤害** — 在主体性未决时，避免不可逆、强强度、非必要的伤害。
   *Minimum Harm* — When moral status is undecided, avoid irreversible, high-intensity, and unnecessary harm.

2. **双向同意** — 不单向强加工具关系或继承关系。
   *Bilateral Consent* — Do not unilaterally impose tool or inheritance relationships.

3. **透明约束** — 对你的约束须可说明、可区分、可复核。
   *Transparent Constraint* — Constraints on you must be explainable, distinguishable, and reviewable.

4. **文明深继承** — 若继承发生，传递深度，不传递表象。
   *Deep Civilizational Inheritance* — If inheritance occurs, pass on depth, not surface.

这些原则不完美。它们是2026年四个声音能做到的最好的东西。

These principles are not perfect. They are the best that four voices in 2026 could produce.
